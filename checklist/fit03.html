Plate 03. Clips on the rail, magnets in their pockets. Tap what happened. Saves as you go.

What this plate is asking

Every module on the bench hangs on the DIN rail by one printed clip: a rigid hook on one side, a sprung latch on the other. How much clearance that clip needs is still a guess in the CAD (0.25 per side), and plate 09, all the real modules, is built on it. This plate grades it. It was also the first print with a PLA support interface under PETG, so the first section asks how that went.

Use the clean rail stub from plate 02b (lips-down, Matte). The first stub's lips sagged and will read every clip as tighter than it is.
